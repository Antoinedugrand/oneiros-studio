Baked before sunrise

Flour, water, salt
& a lot of patience.

Our starter has been fed twice a day since 2016. Every loaf is mixed by hand, shaped at the bench and left to ferment slowly overnight — that’s where the open crumb, the gentle tang and the blistered, crackling crust come from. No shortcuts, no commercial yeast.
